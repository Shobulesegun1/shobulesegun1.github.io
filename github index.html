About

From the floor to the spreadsheet

I didn't start in data. I spent years in logistics, bartending, and machine operation — jobs where you learn fast that decisions are only as good as the numbers behind them. That's what pulled me into analytics: I wanted to be the person building the numbers, not just reacting to them.

I'm now a self-taught, entry-level Data Analyst based in Lagos, building a portfolio project by project — from raw, messy data to Power Query cleaning, pivot tables, and dashboards that hold up to scrutiny. I'm comfortable in advanced Excel and MySQL, and currently expanding into Power BI.

I'm actively looking for a Data Analyst internship or entry-level role where I can keep learning fast and shipping real, useful work.
